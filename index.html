2016 Opened in a Newari house

A café built into the square, not beside it

Kalash Café sits in a restored trader's house two doors from Mangalbazar's main courtyard. We kept the timber lattice windows and the brick floor — you'll drink your coffee where merchants once weighed rice.

We roast in small batches, source vegetables from Lalitpur's morning market, and fold our momo by hand every day before opening.
